Learn Basics

What is a Chit?

A chit is a simple way of planning your monthly savings for a fixed period. You choose a plan based on your monthly budget. You then make the monthly payment for 12 months according to the selected plan.

At the end of the 12-month period, you receive the items and benefits included in your chosen plan.

Example Illustration

If you choose a plan with a monthly payment of ₹800, you pay ₹800 every month for 12 months.

12 months × ₹800
= ₹9,600

At the end of the plan, you receive the products mentioned under that ₹9,600 plan.
