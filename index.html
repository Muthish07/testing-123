WHAT IS A CHIT?

How Monthly Chits Work

A chit is a time-honored savings and credit scheme where members contribute a fixed amount monthly. One member receives the pool amount at agreed intervals until everyone gets their turn.

It combines discipline with benefit—you commit to regular payments while ensuring you receive your target item within a defined timeframe.
